# Book corporate actions from official facts and broker observation

The strategy ledger books a stock split only when a reviewed registry entry (`reference/corporate-actions.json`, pinned by a SHA-256 provenance file) states its ratio and dates **and** the broker already shows exactly the scaled units; it books cash dividends from the strictly parsed TWSE distribution table as a receivable at the ex-date and as cash at the payment date, pending the operator's confirmation of the amount received. It never infers an action from prices or quantity ratios: an unexplained whole multiple, a split the broker does not reflect, or official facts that change after booking stop reconciliation and raise an Operator Queue item. Each phase has a deterministic event key derived from the official facts, so reruns cannot double-book. This trades automatic handling of unforeseen actions for a ledger whose every unit and dividend traces to official evidence; research simulators keep their own archived semantics until a new research revision migrates them.
